# A9 – Pulleys

## Objective
The objective of this assignment is to design a pulley system to transfer power between two parallel axes separated by a center distance of 4-6 inches. It must also meet the following requirements: pulley diameter is 3 in, rotational speed is 1000-2000 RPM, static belt tension between 4 and 6 lbf, minimum safety factor of 3, and the material may be aluminum, ABS or Steel
