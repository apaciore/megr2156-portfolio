# A9 – Pulleys

## Objective
The objective of this assignment is to design a pulley system to transfer power between two parallel axes separated by a center distance of 4-6 inches. It must also meet the following requirements: pulley diameter is 3 in, rotational speed is 1000-2000 RPM, static belt tension between 4 and 6 lbf, minimum safety factor of 3, and the material may be aluminum, ABS or Steel

## Design
![calc1](Screenshot%202026-11-09%20224030.png)

The first thing that I did was design the belt/pulley. I calculated for torques 1 and 2, I researched what the static friction coefficient between polyurethane rubber and 1060 hardened steel is, which I found to be 0.4. The choice for my belt was a circumference of 19 1/4 in, and and a diameter of 1/4 in. 
Link to the belt chosen: https://www.mcmaster.com/3044K606-1835T382/

![calc2](Screenshot%202026-11-09%20224046.png)

The next thing to design in the pulley system was the shaft, which we had to find the minimum diameter of this component as well to support the applied stress. This involved calculating for the shear stress allowed, taking into account the safety factor of 3. The diameter of the 1060 hardened steel shaft that this yielded was 1/4 in, just like the diameter of the belt.

![calc3](Screenshot%202026-11-09%20224116.png)

Following this, the material of the journal bearing had to be calculated for. In order to select a material, I needed to calculate the pressure exerted on the bearing as well as the sliding velocity of it. These two calculations allowed me to find my PV factor in psi*ft/min. Choosing a material that is beneath the stated PV threshold in the machinery handbook, I chose dry graphite as my bearing material. Dry graphite also tends to reduce friction, which supported my decision to choose this.

![calc4](Screenshot%202026-11-09%20224203.png)

The simple design that I opted for features two evenly spaced holes, separated by 5 in per the requirement of 4-6 in spacing, and each at a diameter of 1/4 in. The frame has a thickness of 1/4 in as well, and a width of 1 in. This design allowed for the structure to be fairly compact and not use too much excessive material than what is needed. Upon checking the safety factor that these dimensions yield, it was far greater than the required safety factor of 3, letting me know the design is adequate.

## Communicate
This assignment was great practice for practicing critical thinking in the design process, ensuring that parameters that make the design work are met before anything like physical manufacturing of the design takes place. This practice allows for less materials to be wasted, through both errors of failing designs and creation of designs that are overkill and utilize more material than needed. Therefore, I realized that its always more cost effective to plan comprehensively before moving forward with a design. This assignment took me approximately 3 hours to complete start to finish.
